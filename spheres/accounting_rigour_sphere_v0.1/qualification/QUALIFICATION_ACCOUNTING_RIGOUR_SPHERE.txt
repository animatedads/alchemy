Accounting Rigour Gopher sphere v0.1
====================================

Purpose
-------
LLM-focused money-accounting rigour layered onto LLM Gopher without changing Gopher
engine semantics. The accounting-rigour profile loads core + accounting-rigour and
publishes only article + corpus content. No language-rules are forced: the money
failure modes are semantic (arithmetic-context intent, legal intent, durable store
state), so a syntactic rule would false-positive and is deliberately excluded.

Grounded-in source (observed baseline), all consulted 2026-09-02:
  accounting_core_v0.10.zip  (docs/PERSISTENCE_AND_PRECISION.md,
      SETTLEMENT_ROUNDING_CONTRACT.md, REPORTING_LIFECYCLE_CONTRACT.md,
      REAL_FILING_16024067_QUALIFICATION.md, CHANGELOG.md, QUALIFICATION.md)
