# Legal Effect / Legal Interrogation Gopher Sphere v0.1

Authoritative continuity sphere for the Alchemy Legal Effect domain and the upstream Legal Interrogation authoring boundary.

The sphere documents the separation between immutable source material, clause-by-clause interrogation, structured legal propositions and challenges, source/provision verification, compiler-certified Legal Effect generations, and Institutional Policy publication/deployment. It also records the Intention Service v0.1-dev11 dynamic-discovery integration used to ask the next legally relevant question without making conversational recognition an authority source.

Current grounding is Legal Effect v0.14 (public API `legal.effect/0.10`), Institutional Policy v0.8, Intention Service v0.1-dev11, and `oorexx_legal_interrogation_v0.1-dev6`. Dev6 is qualified on the exact Library ooRexx 5.3.0 r13196 runtime with the real Intention Service dev11 and real Legal Effect v0.14 compiler boundary. The concrete bridge requires verifier-issued source/provision evidence and remains non-authoritative until `LegalRuleCompiler` certifies the generation.

This sphere is documentation/evidence. It does not itself create legal authority, certify a source, publish a policy, or replace professional legal review.
