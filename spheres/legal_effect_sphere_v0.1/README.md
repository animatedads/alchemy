# Legal Effect / Legal Interrogation Gopher Sphere v0.1

Authoritative continuity sphere for the Alchemy Legal Effect domain and the upstream Legal Interrogation authoring boundary.

The sphere documents the separation between immutable source material, clause-by-clause interrogation, structured legal propositions and challenges, compiler-certified Legal Effect generations, and Institutional Policy publication/deployment. It also records the Intention Service v0.1-dev11 dynamic-discovery integration used to ask the next legally relevant question without making conversational recognition an authority source.

Current grounding is Legal Effect v0.14 (public API `legal.effect/0.10`), Institutional Policy v0.8, Intention Service v0.1-dev11, and `oorexx_legal_interrogation_v0.1-dev5`. Legal Interrogation dev5 is contract/standards-qualified and submitted to Alchemy Autobuild; its runtime acceptance receipt was still pending when this sphere was authored.

This sphere is documentation/evidence. It does not itself create legal authority, certify a source, publish a policy, or replace professional legal review.
