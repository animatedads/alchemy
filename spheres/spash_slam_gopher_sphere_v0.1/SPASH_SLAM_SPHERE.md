# SPASH SLAM: identities formed by likeness

SPASH means Spectral Path Association and Spatial Hypotheses. It is the spectral wrestler in the SLAM collective.

OBS denotes a distinct time-frequency observation with sample geometry and processing provenance. LIKE denotes a reproducible, but snapshot-dependent, threshold family. PATH denotes one candidate continuation. SOURCE denotes a physically established emitter and is not inferred by the other three.

Likeness is a weighted demonstration function over normalized spectral shape (50%), relative centre frequency (25%), bandwidth (10%) and optional pitch (15%). When pitch is absent, its weight is excluded and the others are renormalized. Energy is measured but does not define identity. Spectral-vector dimension mismatch is incomparable. Thresholds are not calibrated probabilities.

Candidate paths preserve per-source and per-method continuity; the best path is chosen by length and admitted edge similarity, followed by disjoint alternate paths. Multiple paths can be present at the same time without proving a source count.

In a hypothetical acoustic world, the predicted arrival at a virtual microphone is emission time + 1000 * distance(source, mic) / sound speed. Four virtual positions supply four model predictions, not four independent recorded channels.

Partial replay plans identify exact original source samples, band limits and method IDs. The package does not reconstruct sound, solve room geometry, identify source identity, remove noise, or classify named events.

SLAM owns generic experimental composition vocabulary. Audio V9 owns spectral/acoustic measurement. Media Methodologies owns processing-method provenance. Physics World owns physical simulation. SPASH relates them without transferring their authority.
