# ooRexx Physical Audio Band Gopher Sphere v0.1

Authoritative engineering continuity for the physical-instrument demonstration spanning Physics World, Maths, Rexx-tronics and the standalone Guitar Tab / MIDI Audio frontend.

The central rule is: **the score is not the synthesizer**. ASCII tab and MIDI describe performance intent; Physics owns mechanical state and acoustic radiation; Maths owns generic numerical acceleration; Rexx-tronics owns electrical pickup/amplifier/circuit semantics; Audio owns downstream signal representation/analysis. No layer may silently replace another with a sampled or Python-side synthesizer while claiming physical provenance.
