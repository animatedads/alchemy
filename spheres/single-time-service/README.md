## Fundamental invariants
A unique UTC/POSIX instant is not a civil wall-clock reading, a recurrent intention, or a contextual institution event. TZif rules are explicitly injected; DST gaps and folds must be represented. A rule version and an institutional authority revision are independent provenance axes. A change-impact projection is **not** a durable event.
