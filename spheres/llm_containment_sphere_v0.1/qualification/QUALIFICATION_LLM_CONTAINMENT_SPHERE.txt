LLM Containment Gopher sphere v0.1
==================================

Purpose
-------
LLM-focused containment and honest-evidence guidance layered onto LLM Gopher without
changing engine semantics. The llm-containment profile loads core + llm-containment and
publishes articles, a searchable corpus, and a small mechanically-scoped rule set.

Headline incidents / boundary contracts encoded
-----------------------------------------------
- PokerPromptEnvelope v0.7.38: live prompts admit only public state plus acting-player
  private state; SECRET/INTERNAL and other-player private state fail closed before the
  provider is invoked; prompt audits keep counts/digest, not prompt text.
- Free text is deliberately NOT allowed for live AI speech: frozen dictionary tokens,
  exact Librarian classification, unknown/free-form tokens discarded.
- Observation v0.2: read-only observer stream; mutation-shaped operations fail closed as
  OBSERVATION_MUTATION_FORBIDDEN; passwords remain non-display evidence.
- FlyLo legal-effect model: an AI explanation cannot create, waive or alter an
  entitlement; an unresolved outcome gate becomes REVIEW_REQUIRED, not a guess; absent or
  rejected credential fails closed with no silent model substitution.
- Psychic-poker seeded trajectory (reproducibility) keeps correctness distinct from timing.
