Separation
----------
- accounting-core sphere: conceptual architecture, authority boundaries, scopes, reasoning procedure and reporting evidence model.
- accounting-rigour sphere: implementation hazards/incidents around numeric precision, persistence, replay, rounding and filing no-overclaim.

This split is deliberate; neither sphere replaces executable Accounting Core source/contracts.

Qualification intent
--------------------
The sphere is required to prove useful retrieval for questions an implementation LLM will actually ask:
- context exposes overview + reasoning routine + both corpora;
- exact reasoning routine opens with provenance;
- foreign VAT registration resolves without domicile/local-company assumptions;
- Client Account/regulatory scope is discoverable;
- rehypothecation resolves to policy/evidence extensibility rather than a core-ledger fork;
- 'not zero' resolves filed-account omission discipline;
- REPORT_POPULATION_STALE resolves sealed-report population semantics;
- exact corpus lookup works for tax registration and settlement rounding;
- glossary lookup resolves ReportingSnapshot.

The sphere intentionally publishes no language-rules: its core lessons are semantic and authority-sensitive, so mechanical matching would overclaim.
