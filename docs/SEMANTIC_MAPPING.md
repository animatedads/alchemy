## 2. Fidelity and equivalence rules

- **Lossless or explicit refusal.** X.25 CALL, CUG, Q-bit, D-bit, M-bit, interrupt, reset, PVC state and diagnostic semantics may be lost when crossing to a byte stream. Default is \`UNREPRESENTABLE\`, with explicitly versioned application/profile adapters allowed to translate and document weaker guarantees.
- **Different notions of connection.** SocketSelector's selected transport object, XTP's KEY transaction, XOT's one-TCP-per-VC association, X.25's interface-local logical channel and the gateway's Session ID are different identifiers. Never use one as another's credential, lifecycle identity or deduplication key.
- **Different notions of receipt.** A TCP ACK, XTP CNTL, X.25 D-bit result, Queue Fabric durable insertion, JMS broker disposition and RPC application result prove different events. Preserve each separately with origin and evidence. [ConnectSelector sphere](../spheres/connect_selector_gopher_sphere_v0.1/CONNECT_SELECTOR_SPHERE.md) prohibits guessing a successful side effect after uncertain transport.
- **XOT is not raw X.25-over-UDP.** [RFC 1613](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc1613) requires TCP/1998, four-byte framing and per-VC TCP connection. X.25-over-XTP L4 UDP is a private XTP-based separate protocol.
- **Security is never inferred from payload protocol.** X.25 CUG and X.121 are application/network admission constructs, not cryptographic peer authentication. Ingress identity comes from a qualified authentication layer; permitted opaque content passage requires an explicit policy decision.
- **Native facilities remain typed.** Reverse charging requires separate billing authority; fast select data is at CALL phase; PAD X.3/X.28/X.29 is a separate adapter; X.25 packet size/window and data segmentation do not mean TCP buffers.

## 3. Typed outcome vocabulary (proposed)

\`DENIED_POLICY\`, \`DENIED_UNQUALIFIED_CARRIER\`, \`DENIED_IDENTITY\`, \`DENIED_FACILITY\`, \`DENIED_CUG\`, \`REJECTED_BY_PEER\`, \`REMOTE_DIAGNOSTIC\`, \`TIMED_OUT\`, \`OVERFLOW_OR_BACKPRESSURE\`, \`UNREPRESENTABLE_CONTROL\`, \`RESET_UNREPRESENTABLE\`, \`CARRIER_LOST\`, \`CANCELLED_BY_ADMIN\`, \`UNKNOWN_AFTER_FORWARD\`, \`NORMAL_COMPLETE\`.

Typed outcomes do not substitute for the *actual* X.25 cause/diagnostic octets, XTP DIAG codes or native stream error codes; preserve raw original alongside canonical category and policy/evidence reference.

## 4. Required regression matrix

Synthetic tests: every row's inbound and outbound path; two independent gateways; inbound X.25 CALL policy deny; XTP named-service deny with dev29 DIAG (and old-peer/no-response fallback with explicit local receipt); split TCP reads, stop-reading receiver, both half-close directions, X.25 reset on an active mapped stream, jumbo X.25 message across L2 MTU under enforced segmentation gate, process restart with UNKNOWN, loop detection, policy generation change, simultaneous admission/abort, wall-clock skew; verify no unbounded memory, no silent byte-mixing, no payload in telemetry and no invented delivery assertion.
