# Competitive market price points — 23 October 2026

Prices below are **public list-price reference points**, not feature-equivalent competitive quotes. All figures USD unless explicitly otherwise. Always reverify vendor terms at time of customer quotation.

| Product and tier | Public reference | 20-seat illustration | Key qualification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Google AppSheet Starter | $5/user/month | $100/month | basic app/automation; enterprise features not included |
| Google AppSheet Core | $10/user/month | $200/month | Core may already be in an eligible Google Workspace subscription |
| Google AppSheet Enterprise Plus | $20/user/month | $400/month | enterprise services and governance |
| Microsoft Power Apps Premium | $20/user/month, rising to $22 from 1 January 2027 | $400, then $440/month | existing license rights, Dataverse/data-source and offline specifics need checking |
| Fulcrum Professional | $43/user/month | $860/month | annual subscription; minimum five users |
| Fulcrum Elite | $55/user/month | $1,100/month | SSO/SCIM/API/priority support, annual |
| Appsmith Business | $15/user/month | $300/month | app builder is not a deployed offline field business system |
| Appsmith Enterprise | $2,500/month for a 100-user entry pack | $2,500 published entry | cannot prorate to 20 users as a quoted offer |
| Directus | variable plan, editor and hosting models | no reliable uniform 20-seat quote | count Studio/editor seats separately from external app users, check support/hosting/SSO and license thresholds |

**Example only:** Alchemy NotNotes Field Operations *proposed* at $299/month for a 20-named-user workspace is $101 below 20 Power Apps Premium $20/user seats, and $801 below 20 Fulcrum Elite $55/user seats. This is **arithmetic, not evidence of equivalent functionality or published availability**.

The Gemini/NotNotes buyer interview is a useful requirements source, not a trustworthy price list: a developer-free, LDAP-ready, supported 20-person business system with offline work is a different procurement object from a $20 self-hosted VM. Compare total cost over 12/36 months: subscriptions, setup, migration, support hours, infrastructure, network, backup/restore, training, paid connectors, accepted uptime, mobile/offline conflict resolution, liability and IT staff cost.

**Sales rule:** No "50% cheaper" claim until 20-user offline/identity/retention/support/permissions/capacity/terms are matched and the Alchemy SKU's measured WLU infrastructure and support margin are verified.
