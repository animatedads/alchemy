# Architecture

`FederationBankIntermediaryStaffAuthority` is an authority-composition boundary, not a new source of RID regulatory truth.

1. FederationBank Staff Authority proves that the employee may perform the institutional intermediary operation.
2. The effective-dated representative binding proves which RID firm/representative identity that employee is acting as.
3. The intermediary staff request binds that attribution to the exact product, jurisdiction, activity, advice mode and case.
4. RID still performs its own firm/representative status, permission, appointment, competence, product-approval, journey and customer-evidence checks.

No outcome from this component is a Core Banking command, Ledger instruction, insurance bind, investment execution or mortgage approval.
