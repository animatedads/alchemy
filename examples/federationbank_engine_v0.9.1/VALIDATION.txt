Important boundary:
A terminal timestamp is evidence, not authority. Delayed reconciliation is bounded by a bank-issued, terminal/session/account-specific, single-use authority and a finite bank-side evidence window. The model does not claim that an untrusted terminal clock alone cryptographically proves when cash moved.

All limits, reconciliation windows, clock-skew tolerances, offline authority amounts, fee amounts, FX rates/markups, bureau bands and legal/regulatory content in FederationBankFixtures.cls or the ATM gateway defaults are TEST FIXTURES ONLY.
