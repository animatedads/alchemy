FlyLo retained legal-source representation: UK Regulation (EC) No 261/2004 as applicable in UK law and amended by The Aviation (Consumers) (Amendment) Regulations 2023 (SI 2023/1370).
For a flight departing from a UK airport, UK passenger-rights rules apply. Cancellation triggers a choice between reimbursement of the unused ticket and rerouting. Delay can trigger care; on a long-haul flight the care threshold is four hours. A delay of five hours or more can trigger reimbursement if the passenger chooses not to travel. Arrival delay of three hours or more can trigger Article 7 compensation, subject to the extraordinary-circumstances defence. The 2023 amendment came into force on 14 December 2023 and codified the long-delay compensation principle and the extraordinary-circumstances definition.
Authoritative references: legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2023/1370 ; UK CAA passenger rights guidance.
