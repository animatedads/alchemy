FlyLo retained legal-source representation: U.S. Department of Transportation July 2026 Notification of Enforcement Discretion concerning airline refunds and flight renumbering.
DOT extended through 7 July 2027 a temporary enforcement pause for the specific case where a flight is merely renumbered, the passenger is rebooked under the new flight number, and the itinerary is operated without a significant change or delay. This is enforcement posture, not a rewrite of the underlying Part 260 entitlement, and must not be represented to customers as repeal of the refund rule.
Authoritative reference: U.S. DOT, Airline Refunds and Other Consumer Protections, July 2026.
