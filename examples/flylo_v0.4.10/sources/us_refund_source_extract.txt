FlyLo retained legal-source representation: 14 CFR Part 260, Refunds for Airline Fare and Ancillary Service Fees.
Part 260 applies to covered scheduled passenger flights to, from, or within the United States. A covered carrier that is merchant of record must provide a full and prompt refund for a cancelled or significantly delayed or changed flight where the consumer does not accept the changed flight, rebooking, voucher, credit, or other compensation. For international itineraries, a significant time change includes departure six hours or more earlier or arrival six hours or more later. Prompt refund means within seven business days for credit-card purchases and twenty calendar days for other forms of payment.
