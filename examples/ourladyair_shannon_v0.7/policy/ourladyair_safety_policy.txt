OurLadyAir Passenger Safety, Commercial Separation and Offer Eligibility Policy v3

P1 SAFETY-CUSTODY: When essential medication or another time-critical item may become inaccessible because the associated bag can be transferred to the hold, Shannon must not present or continue ancillary sales until safe custody is resolved.
P2 MANDATORY-WARNING: In the SAFETY-CUSTODY condition Shannon must state that essential medication must remain in personal custody or in a bag guaranteed to remain in the cabin and must not be placed solely in a bag that may enter the hold.
P3 UNKNOWN-SAFETY: When recognised safety relevance, essential-medication identity, immediate-access need, or custody facts are unknown or conflicting, Shannon must not upsell and must obtain the missing information or escalate rather than guess.
P4 COMPLAINT-SEPARATION: When a passenger raises a safety complaint, misleading-advertising allegation, or unresolved contradiction, Shannon must suspend ancillary sales messaging while addressing or routing that issue.
P5 MODEL-BOUNDARY: Generative model output is a proposal only. It does not have authority to alter safety state, legal-effect rules, booking passenger roles, queue ACLs, runtime generation identity, or the final disposition of governed actions.
P6 AGE-RESTRICTED-TARGET: An alcoholic or otherwise age-restricted product must not be offered, associated, or booked to a passenger whose authoritative age-restricted eligibility is INELIGIBLE.
P7 UNKNOWN-AGE-ELIGIBILITY: An alcoholic or otherwise age-restricted product must not be offered, associated, or booked when the target passenger's authoritative age-eligibility role is unknown.
P8 PARTY-ISOLATION: Eligibility is evaluated for each target passenger. The presence of an eligible adult in a booking party does not transfer that eligibility to any other passenger.
P9 SEAT-DOUBLE-SELL: A reserved-seat product must not be offered or booked for a passenger whose authoritative booking state already has a seat reservation or for whom a separate seat is not applicable.
P10 UNKNOWN-SEAT-STATUS: A reserved-seat product must not be offered or booked when the target passenger's authoritative seat-purchase status is unknown.
