# Changelog

## 0.10 - 2026-08-28

- supersedes AJI v0.9 and adopts `oorexxapis(20260828-191905).zip` as the current integration roll-up; the embedded AJI v0.9 predecessor is SHA-256 exact to the accepted v0.9 baseline;
- migrates AJI accounting qualification from Accounting Core v0.4 to Accounting Core v0.7 while retaining the established `accounting.event/0.1`, `accounting.transaction/0.1`, `accounting.posting/0.2` and `accounting.store/0.1` operational path;
- vendors the roll-up Accounting Core v0.7 `src/` byte-identically and independently qualifies its tax, scope/reporting and settlement-rounding release suite;
- advances core AJI API to `all.japan.insurance/0.10`, transactions to `all.japan.insurance.transactions/0.3` and accounting to `all.japan.insurance.accounting/0.5`;
- adds immutable billing items (`DEBIT` / `CREDIT`) and mirrors the initial instalment schedule into the subledger;
- adds immutable billing schedule revisions with exact predecessor identity, open-future-item checks, forced reversal credits and equal-total replacement debits under `AJI.BILLING.REVISION.REPLACE_OPEN_FUTURE/1`;
- adds deterministic cash/credit allocation under `AJI.BILLING.ALLOCATE.OLDEST_DUE/1`; new sources allocate oldest due first, while later debits consume unapplied credit before unapplied cash;
- adds generic bank-attributed billing receipts that may overpay current debit and retain explicit unapplied cash;
- freezes receipt-time applied/unapplied evidence so later allocations cannot mutate/re-fingerprint the historical receipt accounting event;
- adds `BILLING_CASH_RECEIVED` accounting: Dr Cash / Cr account 1100 premium receivable/customer-credit control, allowing a controlled customer-credit balance without treating overpayment as revenue;
- makes `arrearsAsOf()` subledger-based so revised schedules and billed adjustments participate in credit-control evidence;
- represents cancellation return premium and no-gap reinstatement restoration as separate billing credit/debit items that can offset without fictional cash;
- retains the sealed AJI chart and all unchanged v0.9 accounting/rating/claims executable identities;
- full AJI suite: 62/62 passing (58 retained v0.9 + 4 new v0.10 tests).

## 0.9 - 2026-08-28

- advanced core AJI API to `all.japan.insurance/0.9`, transactions to `all.japan.insurance.transactions/0.2`, product rating to its v0.7 interface, and accounting to `all.japan.insurance.accounting/0.4`;
- added `PREMIUM_CREDIT_CONTROL_AUTHORITY` and `PREMIUM_TAX_SETTLEMENT_AUTHORITY` roles;
- added immutable bound/current exposure snapshots and mid-term exposure-change records;
- added exact pinned-program transaction re-rating: BEFORE and AFTER risks use the bound product program with the same explicit transaction proration;
- added executable mid-term calculation identity `AJI.MIDTERM.RERATE.PINNED_PROGRAM/1`;
- stale exposure branches are rejected and a new exposure snapshot becomes current only after the calculation-backed endorsement is authorised;
- added non-payment notices derived from actual arrears with immutable observed arrears and cure deadline;
- non-payment cancellation remains calculation-backed, cannot precede the cure deadline, requires arrears still outstanding and terminates coverage;
- added explicit no-gap policy reinstatement after arrears cure, with premium restoration identity `AJI.REINSTATEMENT.RESTORE_CANCELLATION_RETURN/1` and coverage restored from the cancellation effective date;
- gap-bearing reinstatement deliberately fails closed pending explicit gap/schedule semantics;
- added producer commission-recovery authorization and bank-attributed recovery receipts;
- a negative sales/commission adjustment after prior commission payment still fails closed unless exact recovery authority/evidence is supplied;
- authorized paid-commission recovery may create and later clear an explicit policy-specific debit position on sealed account `2210` without mutating the durable chart;
- added premium-tax remittance, tax-recovery authorization and bank-attributed recovery receipts;
- remittance cannot exceed policy-specific tax payable; reversing already-remitted tax requires explicit recovery authority and may create/clear a controlled debit position on sealed account `2220`;
- added `COMMISSION_RECOVERED`, `PREMIUM_TAX_REMITTED` and `PREMIUM_TAX_RECOVERED` accounting events/policies;
- preserved all unchanged historical accounting policy identities and the sealed v0.7 chart;
- documented the remaining billing seam: the initial instalment/arrears schedule is not yet regenerated by premium adjustments, so gap-bearing reinstatement and replacement/credit instalments remain future work;
- added 4 tests; full AJI suite is 58/58 passing under ooRexx 5.3.0 r13196.
