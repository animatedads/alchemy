# F11 continuous vehicle experiment

Authoritative vertical geometry: road z=0.0 m; F11 floor z=3.0 m; ceiling z=5.2 m; FC/FD/internal source z=4.8 m; vehicle acoustic source z=0.5 m; vehicle height 1.5 m and length 4.0 m.

Vehicle speed is 40 km/h = 11.111111111111111 m/s. The vehicle is continuous. Five fixed road crossing points are sampled, 6 m apart, hence 0.54 s apart.

LTR: x=-4,2,8,14,20 m at t=0,0.54,1.08,1.62,2.16 s.
RTL: x=20,14,8,2,-4 m at t=0,0.54,1.08,1.62,2.16 s.

Experiment families:
1. no stationary noise + car RTL
2. no stationary noise + car LTR
3. stationary noise at each grid point + no car
4. stationary noise at each grid point + car RTL
5. stationary noise at each grid point + car LTR

Vehicle source is 78 dB SPL total @1 m split by acoustic power: 125 Hz 30%, 1000 Hz 60%, 4000 Hz 10%. Each microphone receives independent arrival, level and Doppler frequency calculations.
