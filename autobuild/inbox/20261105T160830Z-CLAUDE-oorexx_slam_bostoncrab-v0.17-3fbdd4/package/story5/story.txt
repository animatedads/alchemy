Barnaby stood up and stretched his front legs.
He sniffed the cool morning air.
A small brown toad sat by the shed.
The toad stayed still on the damp moss.
Its bumpy skin matched the dark ground.
Barnaby took two steps toward the wooden fence.
A shiny black beetle crawled up a log.
The beetle carried a heavy piece of bark.
Barnaby pushed his nose into a leaf pile.
A hidden acorn sprouted a tiny green stem.
Root threads grew down into the wet soil.
Barnaby walked over to the vegetable garden.
A fat caterpillar chewed a green tomato leaf.
It left tiny holes in the soft leaf.
Pip dropped a pinecone from the high branch.
The pinecone landed with a quiet thud.
Pip chattered from the top of the tree.
Barnaby looked up and wagged his tail.
He trotted back to the kitchen door.
