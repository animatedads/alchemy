Barnaby trotted to the old wooden birdbath.
He stood on his hind legs to look.
Clear rainwater filled the stone bowl.
A blue bird flew down from the sky.
It splashed its wings in the cold water.
Water drops sprayed onto Barnaby's nose.
The bird shook its wet feathers dry.
It picked up a red berry from a bush.
The bird flew back up to the fence.
Barnaby walked over to the wooden compost box.
He sniffed the warm, dark heap of leaves.
A shiny red ladybird crawled onto a twig.
It opened its wings and flew away.
Barnaby lay down on the soft grass.
He watched Pip run along the top fence rail.
Pip carried a dry brown leaf in his mouth.
Barnaby rested his chin on his front paws.
