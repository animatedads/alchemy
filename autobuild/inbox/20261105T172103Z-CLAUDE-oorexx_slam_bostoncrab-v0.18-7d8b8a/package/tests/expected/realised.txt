Barnaby was a big floppy dog.
Barnaby's job was to watch the back yard.
Every morning at eight o'clock, Arthur opened the door.
Barnaby ran out onto his green lawn.
Barnaby sniffed the air.
Barnaby saw Pip.
Pip was a grey squirrel.
Pip lived in his tall oak tree.
Pip was not scared of Barnaby.
"Woof!" Barnaby barked.
"Get off my lawn!" Barnaby barked.
Pip sat on a branch.
Pip held his brown nut.
"I am not on your lawn," Pip said.
"I am in my tree!" Pip said.
Pip dropped his nut.
Thud!
Pip's nut hit Barnaby's lawn near Barnaby's paws.
Barnaby did not bite Pip's nut.
Barnaby picked Pip's nut up with his mouth.
Barnaby set Pip's nut near Pip's tree.
"Your nut is here," Barnaby said.
"Now I must do my job," Barnaby said.
Barnaby walked around the yard.
Barnaby checked under the shed.
Barnaby checked the big red hose.
Barnaby pushed three brown leaves into a pile with his nose.
Pip ran down the fence.
"Why do you push the leaves?" Pip asked.
"To keep the yard clean," Barnaby said.
Pip grabbed a dry yellow leaf.
Pip threw the dry yellow leaf up in the air.
The dry yellow leaf landed on Barnaby's lawn with a bounce.
"Throwing leaves is fun!" Pip squeaked.
Barnaby looked at a yellow leaf on his lawn.
Barnaby blew the air out of his nose.
Puff!
The yellow leaf flew into the air.
The yellow leaf landed on Barnaby's nose.
Pip laughed.
"Look at you!" Pip said.
"You can play!" Pip said.
Barnaby looked at the yellow leaf on his nose.
Barnaby's tail began to wag.
"Barnaby! Come eat!" Arthur called out from the house.
Barnaby shook the yellow leaf off his nose.
Barnaby looked up at Pip.
"See you tomorrow, Pip," Barnaby said.
"See you tomorrow, Barnaby," Pip called back.
