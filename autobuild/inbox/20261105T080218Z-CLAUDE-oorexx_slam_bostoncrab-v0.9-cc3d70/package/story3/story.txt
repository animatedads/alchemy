Barnaby was a hound with long ears and a super-nose. A dog’s nose is amazing—it can smell things humans cannot smell at all!
Every morning, Barnaby explored the back yard to see what was happening.
First, he sniffed the dark dirt under the trees. He saw a little pink earthworm wiggle into the soil. Earthworms are the yard's tiny gardeners. They eat old leaves and dirt, and as they dig, they make holes that let air and water reach the roots of the grass.
Next, Barnaby walked across the lawn. The grass was wet with tiny drops of water called dew. Dew happens at night when the cool air turns water vapor into real water drops on the leaves.
Chitter-chatter!
Barnaby looked up at the oak tree. Pip the squirrel was holding a brown acorn.
"I am hiding my food!" Pip chattered.
In the autumn, squirrels bury nuts in the ground so they have food to eat during the cold winter. If a squirrel forgets where it buried a nut, that nut can sprout and grow into a brand-new tree!
Barnaby lay down in a warm patch of sunlight. His big ears twitched. Dogs have super-hearing, too. He could hear the buzzing of a bee visiting a flower to collect sweet nectar.
The yard was full of busy animals, insects, and plants—and Barnaby loved checking on every single one.
