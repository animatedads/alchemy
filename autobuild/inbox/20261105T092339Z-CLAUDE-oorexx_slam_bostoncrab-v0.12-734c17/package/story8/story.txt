# Part 1
Barnaby trotted out into the back yard.
Arthur opened the kitchen door at eight.
Barnaby ran across the green grass.
Pip the squirrel sat on a tree branch.
Pip held a small brown acorn in his paws.
"Woof!" barked Barnaby.
Pip scampered along the branch to the trunk.
Pip ran down the trunk to the grass.
Pip placed the acorn on the soft dirt.
"That is acorn number one," Pip chattered.
Pip scampered back up to the high branch.
Pip grabbed another acorn from a leaf cluster.
He ran down the trunk to the grass.
Pip set down the acorn near the first.
"That is acorn number two," Pip said.
Pip ran back up the trunk to the branch.
He picked a shiny brown acorn.
Pip ran down the trunk to the ground.
He put down acorn number three.
Barnaby watched Pip collect the nuts.
Pip ran up the tree again.
He grabbed a fat acorn from the twig.
Pip ran down the trunk to the grass.
He placed acorn number four in the line.
Pip climbed up the tree trunk.
He picked a smooth acorn from the branch.
Pip ran down the trunk to the grass.
He set down acorn number five.
Pip scampered up the tree again.
He picked a small acorn near a leaf.
Pip ran down the trunk to the dirt.
He placed acorn number six in the row.
Pip ran up the trunk to the branch.
He grabbed a heavy acorn with his paws.
Pip ran down the trunk to the grass.
He put down acorn number seven.
Pip climbed back up to the high branch.
He picked a round acorn from the cluster.
Pip ran down the trunk to the dirt.
He placed acorn number eight in the line.
Pip scampered up the tree trunk.
He picked a dark acorn from a twig.
Pip ran down the trunk to the grass.
He set down acorn number nine.
Pip ran back up to the branch.
He picked a tiny acorn from the oak.
Pip ran down the trunk to the ground.
He placed acorn number ten on the soil.
Pip climbed up the tree again.
He picked a dry acorn near the top.
Pip ran down the trunk to the grass.
He put down acorn number eleven.
Pip scampered up the trunk one last time.
He picked a bright acorn from the branch.
Pip ran down the trunk to the grass.
He set down acorn number twelve.
Barnaby looked at the row of acorns.
"Twelve acorns!" Barnaby woofed politely.
Pip buried all twelve acorns in the dirt.
Barnaby rested his chin on his paws.
# Part 2 (after Gemini's answer: 7 remain)
Barnaby nudged one of the seven remaining acorns with his nose.
"You left seven nuts on the grass," Barnaby said.
Pip scampered over to the seven acorns.
Pip picked up the sixth acorn with his paws.
Pip ran across the lawn to the wooden shed.
Pip dug a small hole in the dark dirt.
Pip pushed the acorn into the hole.
Pip covered the acorn with a brown leaf.
Pip scampered back to the lawn.
Six acorns remained on the green grass.
Pip picked up the seventh acorn.
Pip ran across the grass to the garden fence.
Pip dug a hole under a wooden post.
Pip pushed the acorn into the soft earth.
Pip covered the hole with fresh dirt.
Pip scampered back to the lawn.
Five acorns remained on the green grass.
Pip picked up the eighth acorn.
Pip ran over to the flowerbed.
Pip buried the acorn under a purple flower.
Pip scampered back to the grass.
Four acorns remained in the row.
Pip picked up the ninth acorn.
Pip ran to the big stone birdbath.
Pip buried the acorn in the cool soil.
Pip scampered back to the lawn.
Three acorns remained on the grass.
Pip picked up the tenth acorn.
Pip ran over to the compost box.
Pip buried the acorn under two dry leaves.
Pip scampered back to the lawn.
Two acorns remained on the grass.
Pip picked up the eleventh acorn.
Pip ran to the root of the oak.
Pip buried the acorn near the trunk.
Pip scampered back to the row.
One acorn remained on the green grass.
Pip picked up the twelfth acorn.
Pip ran to the corner of the yard.
Pip buried the final acorn in the dirt.
Zero acorns remained on the grass.
Pip sat on the ground and patted his paws.
"All twelve acorns are buried!" Pip chattered.
Barnaby wagged his tail at the clean lawn.
