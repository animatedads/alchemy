Barnaby trotted out into the back yard.
The morning grass felt cool and wet.
Tiny water drops sat on the blades.
Barnaby put his nose down to the dirt.
He smelled a pink worm under the ground.
The worm wiggled through the dark soil.
It pushed small air holes into the earth.
Barnaby walked over to the tall oak tree.
Pip the squirrel sat on a high branch.
Pip held a brown acorn in his paws.
Pip scampered along the branch to the trunk.
He ran down the trunk to the ground.
Pip dug a small hole in the dirt.
He pushed the acorn into the dark ground.
He covered the hole with three dead leaves.
Barnaby watched Pip hide the acorn.
Pip ran up the trunk to the branch.
A yellow bee landed on a purple flower.
The bee drank sweet nectar from the blossom.
Yellow pollen dust stuck to the bee's legs.
The bee flew to another purple flower.
Barnaby lay down in the warm sunlight.
He twitched his big ears at the buzzing.
Barnaby closed his eyes and rested his paws.
