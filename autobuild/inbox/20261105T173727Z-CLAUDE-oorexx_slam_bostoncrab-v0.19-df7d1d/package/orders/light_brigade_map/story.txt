# Situation
The British guns were in the redoubts on the Causeway Heights.
The Russian cavalry were taking the British guns from the redoubts.
The Russian guns were at the end of the North Valley.
The Russian gunners were beside the Russian guns.
The Russian cavalry were enemies.
The Russian gunners were enemies.
The British cavalry were in the North Valley.
Lucan commanded the British cavalry.
Raglan was on the Sapoune Heights.
Lucan was in the North Valley.
# Order — Raglan to Lucan
Lord Raglan wishes the cavalry to advance rapidly to the front.
Follow the enemy and try to prevent the enemy carrying away the guns.
Troop Horse Artillery may accompany.
French cavalry is on your left.
Immediate.
