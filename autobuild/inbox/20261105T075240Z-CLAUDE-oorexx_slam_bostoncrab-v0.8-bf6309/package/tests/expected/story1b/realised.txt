Barnaby was a big floppy dog.
Barnaby's job was to watch the back yard.
Every morning at eight o'clock, Arthur opened the door.
Barnaby ran out onto the green lawn.
Barnaby sniffed the cool air.
Pip sat on a tree branch.
"Woof!" Barnaby barked.
Pip held a brown nut in his paws.
"I am in my tree!" Pip said.
Pip dropped the nut to the lawn.
Thud!
The nut hit the lawn near Barnaby's paws.
Barnaby picked the nut up with his mouth.
Barnaby set the nut near Pip's tree.
Barnaby walked around the yard.
Barnaby checked under the shed.
Barnaby checked the big red hose.
Barnaby pushed three brown leaves into a pile.
Pip leaped to the fence from his tree.
Pip ran to the lawn down the fence.
"Why do you push the leaves?" Pip asked.
"To keep the yard clean," Barnaby said.
Pip grabbed a dry yellow leaf.
Pip threw the leaf up into the air.
Pip landed on the lawn with a bounce.
Barnaby blew the air out of his nose.
Puff!
The leaf flew into the air.
The leaf landed on Barnaby's nose.
Pip laughed.
Barnaby looked at the leaf on his nose.
Barnaby's tail began to wag.
"Barnaby! Come eat!" Arthur called out.
Barnaby shook the leaf off his nose.
Barnaby looked up at Pip.
"See you tomorrow, Pip," Barnaby said.
"See you tomorrow, Barnaby," Pip called back.
