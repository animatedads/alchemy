# Brand Narrative Directive architecture

Brand Narrative Directive is a connector-neutral, NO_DISPATCH handoff between the Brand evidence/governance line and an independently owned runtime narrative authority.

It does not publish, send, mutate prompts, assign customer treatment, choose channels, or activate Dynamic Story. A governed Brand decision becomes an immutable directive; every effectful handoff requires fresh host authority verification bound to the exact Governance decision and, where present, exact Institutional Policy selection.

## Adjacent evidence boundaries

Communications Discovery supplies detached editorial narrative-evidence review. Current dev14 semantics require the external story candidate and visible Librarian terminal revision to be re-evaluated per review request; retraction and supersession are external HOLD states. Brand Narrative Directive may retain the returned assessment as an immutable review snapshot, but does not convert that snapshot into truth, claim approval or publication authority.

Librarian supplies evidence, never Brand or publication authority. Dev6 correction/retraction/conflict lineage is preserved without treating retraction as falsity. Dev7 declared source lineage adds relationships such as `SHARED_DECLARED_ORIGIN`, but a common declared root is not verified provenance and different roots do not establish independence. The directive therefore records origin authority as `DECLARED_NOT_VERIFIED` and keeps bias, causation and editorial independence unestablished.

The strongest handoff state remains `READY_FOR_EXTERNAL_AUTHORITY_REVIEW`. That is a workflow boundary, not permission to activate or publish a narrative.
