metre: the name of one part of a way from one place to an other place; you count a way with this part
metres: more than one metre
meter: the same as metre
length: the count of metres in a way from one place to an other place
side: a part at the end of a thing; a thing can have more than one side
sides: more than one side
picture: a list of things, each at a place; for each place there are two numbers: the length from one side, then the length from an other side
top: the side of a picture where you start the count of the second number
on: at a thing, as a part of it, or at the top of it
across: the way from the side of a picture where you start the count of the first number, to the side at the other end
left: the side of a picture where you start the count of the first number; when you go a way, turn the picture so you go to the top of it, then the side of you that is on the left of the picture is your left
right-side: the side of a picture at the other end of the count across from the left; when you go a way, the side of you that is not your left is your right
turn: put a thing in an other way, at the same place
ahead: at a place on the way you go now, after where you are
front: the side of a thing that is ahead of it; in front of you is ahead of you
behind: at a place on the way you go now, before where you are; behind is the other of ahead
down: ahead on a way, from the start of the way to the end of it
wide: a name for the length from the left side of a way to the right side of it
long: a name for the length from the start of a way to the end of it
home: a place for a person to be in, with parts in it
room: a part of a home, with a side at each end; a person can be in it
rooms: more than one room
wall: a side of a room; you can not go from one room to an other room at a wall
door: a part of a wall where you can go from one room to the next room
doors: more than one door
hall: a room that is more long than wide, with doors to other rooms; you go down a hall from one room to an other room
hallway: the same as hall
living: a name before room; a living room is the room of a home where all the persons of the home can be at the same time
