01 / THE SUBJECT

Alchemy explores intention-aware services, structured language, governed automation, model interoperability and intelligent coordination with independent business authority.

01 / 03

Intentions are first-class

A request may state a desired outcome without specifying every intermediate step. Intention-aware software can reason about goals and constraints while preserving explicit admission, authorisation and execution boundaries.

  • Goal and constraint modelling
  • Decision evidence
  • Authorised execution
02 / 03

Structure beneath language

Structured utterances, communications discovery and narrative services can retain why something was said, which evidence supports it, and which claims may be presented to a given audience.

  • Source lineage
  • Communication purpose
  • Brand and policy boundaries
03 / 03

Choice without lock-in

LLMs and other specialists can be providers of capability. The surrounding object, service and governance contracts determine how their output is used; no model receives blanket authority because it sounds convincing.

  • Provider-neutral integration
  • Machine-readable service contracts
  • Human review where required
