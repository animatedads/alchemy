NoSQLServer v0.40 validation
============================

Focus
-----
SQL grammar whitespace normalization at the native SQL façade.

Behaviour
---------
- Runs of space, TAB, LF, and CRLF outside quoted strings are normalized to one
  grammar space before dispatch/parsing.
- Quoted text is preserved, including repeated spaces and doubled quote escapes.
- Exact MariaDB client shape `SELECT *\nFROM ...\nWHERE ...` now works.
- GROUP BY and ORDER BY may be split across lines/tabs.
- UPDATE/SET/WHERE and DELETE/FROM/WHERE may be split across lines/tabs.
- v0.39 positional and explicit-column INSERT forms continue to work.
- No SQL rewriting occurs in the wire shim.

Capabilities
------------
- SQL_FLEXIBLE_WHITESPACE
- INSERT_FLEXIBLE_WHITESPACE
- INSERT_POSITIONAL_VALUES
