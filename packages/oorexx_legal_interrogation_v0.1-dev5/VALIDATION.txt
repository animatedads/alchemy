Dev5 correction
---------------
Inspection of the actual Alchemy GitHub `packages/oorexx_intention_service` v0.1-dev11 source showed that IntentionService owns
`discoveryGeneration` and advances it only when the concatenated discovery snapshot source/revision signature changes. dev4 used
LegalInterrogationIntentionPort's per-call discovery counter as the snapshot revision, which made an unchanged legal state appear
changed on every refresh. dev5 corrects the snapshot revision to the LegalInterrogationSession authoring revision plus closure state.

Expected invariants
-------------------
- repeated refresh of unchanged legal state leaves IntentionService~discoveryGeneration unchanged;
- a structured authoring mutation advances LegalInterrogationSession~revision;
- the next Intention Service refresh then advances its discovery generation;
- service-copied IntentionSurfaceAdvertisement objects carry the service-owned generation;
- a surface discovered before the authoring mutation remains unusable and fails STALE_DISCOVERY;
- discovery availability remains ADVISORY and is not legal authority.
