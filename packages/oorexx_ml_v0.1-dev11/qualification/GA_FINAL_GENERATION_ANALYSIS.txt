GA final-generation correction (v0.1-dev3)

Observed dev2 integration pattern:
- caller loop invoked MLGeneticAlgorithm~step(evaluator) N times;
- step evaluated population generation G, bred and committed generation G+1;
- the last G+1 population was therefore not scored unless another step/evaluation followed.

Correction:
- low-level step semantics remain explicit and return newPopulationEvaluated=false;
- evaluateCurrent(evaluator) evaluates/publishes the current population without breeding;
- run(evaluator, breedingSteps) performs breeding steps and then evaluateCurrent before return;
- MLGABudgetPlan budgets the initial and final evaluated populations, not only breeding calls.

Regression test_ga_final_generation_scored.rex:
- one breeding step starts at generation 0 and returns generation 1;
- evaluator sees both G0-* and G1-* genomes;
- exactly 8 fitness calls for a 4-individual population;
- evolution registry contains two published evaluated generations.
