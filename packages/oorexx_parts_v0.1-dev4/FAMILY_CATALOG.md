# Parameterised family extension

The dev4 package retains `PartDefinition` and `CommonParts` as its catalogue
authority. `CommonPartFamilies` constructs those same definitions for reusable
families: M2–M12 fasteners, nuts, washers, bearings, shafts, springs, plates,
gears, wire, DIP packages and batteries.

Dimensions use Units quantities. Derived values include bolt head-shear
capacity, spring rate, gear pitch diameter, wire resistance and stock mass.
Material IDs are resolved through the independent Materials package; behaviour
remains owned by Physics World, Physical Manufacturing and Rexx-tronics.
