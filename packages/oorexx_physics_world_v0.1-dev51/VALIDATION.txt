Honest dev10 boundary
--------------------
Driven radiation is a compact monopole-equivalent finite patch with retained
mechanical history and direct-path acoustic propagation. It is not yet a finite
piston directivity/baffle/diffraction solver and acoustic loading does not push back
onto the diaphragm.

Thermal is a lumped-capacitance explicit solver with constant supplied properties.
It is not a continuum heat-equation mesh, phase-change solver, CFD-derived natural
convection solver or temperature-dependent material engine.

Earlier non-claims remain: automatic glass fracture, arbitrary mesh contact,
general CFD beyond the explicit 1-D/2-D shallow-water tank models, fully 3-D free surfaces, spill/mass loss, field-level electromagnetics, granular mechanics and combustion/blast.
