Read `CLAUDE.md` and `AGENTS.md` in the Computer project before invoking effectful operations. Use `Computer help` and `Computer job help` to discover current syntax. Present exact argv, target, option order, exception policies and pending confirmations to the operator. A `job run` is only a preview until independently authorised. After execution use `Computer job result RUN_ID` and `Computer job explain RUN_ID`; never declare success merely because a child exited zero if any expected exception was raised or its observation was incomplete.
