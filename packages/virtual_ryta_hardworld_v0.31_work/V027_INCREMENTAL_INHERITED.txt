----------------

this unchanged Librarian/Algorithm Relation path can exceed the outer execution

Security qualification
----------------------
V3 proves authenticity and ordering of the records that are observed. A local
MAC chain by itself cannot prove that a valid older prefix was not restored.
Rollback/truncation detection therefore requires a separately retained trusted
or monotonic checkpoint. QueueAuthorityExecutionLedger exposes checkpoint text
and can require an expected checkpoint at open; v0.27 does not claim the local
ledger is independently rollback-proof.
