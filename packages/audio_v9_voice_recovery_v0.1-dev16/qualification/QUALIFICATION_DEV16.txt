DEV16 SEMANTIC INCREMENT
  * Adds AudioV9PropagationReconstruction.cls.
  * Audio evidence establishes acoustic propagation/reflection paths.
  * Video/vehicle geometry may corroborate an existing audio observation but
    cannot create acoustic fusion authority by itself.
  * Low-level whisper gain reference is updated only from non-loud,
    non-transient windows.
  * Transient vehicle/reflection gain does not redefine source loudness.
  * Loud/impulsive observations are explicitly marked for a separate stem and
    do not reset whisper AGC/reference state.
  * Compatible static/transient reflections may join a fusion plan only after
    passing the audio gate. Reflected contribution is bounded and all weights
    are normalized rather than blindly summed.
  * Adds correlate_vehicle_reflections.rex as the ingestion seam for pending
    video-processing evidence.
  * No native DSP, loud-echo v1/v2/v3, clock authority, TF-mask policy,
    ML dev11, ML Graph dev5, corpus authority, or campaign evidence was changed.
  * Final multi-path audio rendering is intentionally deferred until real
    video/audio reflector evidence is available for tuning/qualification.
