Real-survey trigger
-------------------
See DEV11_REAL_SURVEY_FINDINGS.txt.  The frozen eight-hour dev11 survey contains
467 events but all are FC-triggered; the largest FD peak is below dev11's shared
0.50 absolute floor.  74/402 high-cross rows hit a +/-1 ms family boundary and
family score overlap is widespread.

V2 semantic repair
------------------
* FC and FD candidate admission is independent and relative to each feed's own
  guarded one-second RMS baseline.
* A very low absolute numerical safety floor (default 0.001) remains configurable.
* A refractory cluster chooses the largest peak-to-own-baseline ratio, not the
  largest raw amplitude.
* trigger_feed is separate from cross-feed acoustic leader.
* leader is UNRESOLVED below the strong cross-score threshold.
* best_echo_feed is separate from best_echo_family.
* every feed/family pair retains measured lag samples, measured lag ms, score,
  and search-boundary contact.
* best/second score margin, strong-family count, strong feed/family count and
  boundary-hit count are first-class evidence.
* default family radius is widened from +/-1 ms to +/-4 ms, still bounded.
* overlapping family windows are intentionally retained as competing evidence.
* pattern_hint requires strong local echo and strong cross-feed evidence and is
  still diagnostic only.
