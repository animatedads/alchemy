Audio V9 Voice Recovery v0.1-dev10 qualification
================================================

Purpose
-------
Move into full-campaign processing without making unresolved global file-edge
clock calibration a blocking dependency.

Processing contract
-------------------
* Final canonical timeline: exactly 18 h at 8 kHz = 518,400,000 samples.
* Seven logical worker cores remain the authoritative ownership partition.
* Each owned piece is decoded with 4 s context on both sides.
* Local FC/FD spatial lag is rescanned from the actual audio using 8 s windows
  advancing every 2 s. Global unresolved file-edge corrections are not invented.
* AudioV9SoftReconstructionPolicy performs soft dual-feed fusion, dominant-feed
  fallback at low coherence, up to +32 dB bounded gain, impulse gain hold and
  peak guard. Low energy never means non-speech.
* Context is cropped away before a chunk becomes authoritative; only owned core
  samples are retained.
* Chunk parts are independently hashed and resume-safe. A verified completed
  part is not decoded or analyzed again after restart.
* Worker voice.f32 outputs are exact float masters. The final campaign is
  encoded to OGG exactly once after all seven logical worker hashes and sample
  counts verify.
* The first full processing pass intentionally omits the expensive acoustic
  graph / TF-mask stage. Those remain optional second-pass enhancement evidence.
