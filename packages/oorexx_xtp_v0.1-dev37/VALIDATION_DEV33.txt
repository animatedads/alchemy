6. Behaviour changes to note
  - Every session open is now rate limited per sending host (200/s, burst 400, 2,000/s in
    total, all adjustable), clear opens included.
  - Once any service has a profile, unprotected sessions may hold at most half of a
    listener's table and backlog.
  - A protected service's messages always go as FIRST+DATA streams.
  - LDAP refresh: entries are checked one by one; a bad entry is reported in WARNINGS
    instead of failing the whole refresh.
