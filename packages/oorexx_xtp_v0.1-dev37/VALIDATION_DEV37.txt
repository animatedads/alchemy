5. Behaviour changes to note
  - Route evidence for an address without XTP_MODE=session is OPENABLE 0, REASON message_mode.
  - Re-installing a key with the same secret but another principal, protection or suite ends
    the contexts under it, as a new secret does.
  - Listener::close() and MulticastListener::close() no longer free the descriptor; it is freed
    when the listener is destroyed. A receive() waiting on L2 (AF_PACKET) stops at its next
    receive timeout, not at once.
