# Transient reflector contract

For source `S`, receiver `M`, and tracked surface point `R(t)`, dev19 predicts:

`excess(t) = |S-R(t)| + |R(t)-M| - |S-M|`

`delay_samples(t) = excess(t) * sample_rate / sound_speed`

This prediction is context, not evidence authority.  H1 is admitted only when
an independent audio path follows the predicted delay.  High vehicle energy
without speech-coherent path support is H3; direct-path loss without an admitted
reflection is H2.  Everything else remains H0.

An AVAILABLE virtual-microphone candidate means that measured acoustic copies
may be delay-corrected and assessed as an additional physical observation.  It
does not authorize transcription, generative restoration, automatic source
identity, or replacement of frozen evidence.
