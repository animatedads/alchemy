TRUTHFUL DEVELOPMENT LIMITS
----------------------------
Not claimed in v0.1-dev9:
- complete OpenLDAP syncrepl provider/consumer compatibility beyond the qualified RFC 4533 conversation;
- cancellation of arbitrary in-flight point operations: the qualified cancellable outstanding class is refreshAndPersist Search;
- arbitrary concurrent point-operation execution; message-ID multiplexing is qualified while `concurrentDispatch=false`;
- automatic application of generic LDAP Sync content as the richer platform
  semantic peer-change/provenance contract;
- schema/matching-rule-aware DN comparison beyond `oorexx-ldap-dn-case-ignore/0.1`;
- LDAP `extensibleMatch` and a general matching-rule engine;
- general schema enforcement, dynamic schema mutation or a production ooRexx OID allocation claim;
- server-side sorting / VLV-style controls beyond RFC 2696 simple paging;
- SASL mechanisms beyond the TLS-required reference PLAIN provider;
- mutual-TLS client-certificate policy qualification;
- durable-log checkpoint/compaction;
- atomicity across a multi-change higher-level operation such as key rollover;
- AD/Entra domain-controller semantics, Kerberos, DNS integration or Microsoft
  replication protocols.

RESULT
------
PASS — v0.1-dev9 retains the neutral Identity Directory, `.DateTime` lifecycle,
durable NoSQL store, Secret Broker, Access/Permission, TLS/SASL, Content Sync,
Cancel/message multiplexing and paging boundaries and adds standards-oriented LDAP
DN parsing, richer RFC 4515 filter semantics and real read-only subschema
discovery. DN/filter/schema behavior remains an LDAP edge concern: stable platform
identity and policy stay neutral. Schema-aware matching rules, extensibleMatch,
general schema enforcement, full OpenLDAP syncrepl interoperability, arbitrary
point-operation cancellation/concurrency and AD/Entra domain-controller semantics
remain deliberately outside the claim.
