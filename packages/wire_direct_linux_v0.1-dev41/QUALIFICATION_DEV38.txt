Wire Direct Linux dev38 — single-method draft boundary
Reject subsequent ooRexx package directives in editor-returned source before mutating a draft. This prevents a pasted second ::method, ::class, or ::requires from being silently accepted as the selected method body. The guard checks directive-shaped lines and is not a full Rexx parser. Editor integration remains detached; GTK4 native compilation and real ooRexx execution are not claimed.
